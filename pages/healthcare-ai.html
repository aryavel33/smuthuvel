Healthcare

Bringing the contact center into the EHR, where care teams already work

At Cisco, I treated healthcare as its own product problem: clinicians, nurses and care teams work from the electronic health record, not a CRM desktop. That insight shaped the Epic CTI connector and AI agent automation with Epic.

CompanyCisco · Webex Contact Center
My roleProduct Manager II, CX AI Integrations
FocusHealthcare vertical · EHR integrations · AI agents
Public launchesEpic CTI connector · AI agent automation with Epic
ShowcasedHIMSS 2026, Cisco AI Pavilion and Webex CX demo booth

The insight

In most industries, contact-center agents work in a CRM. In healthcare, the people serving patients work in the EHR. A healthcare contact-center product has to meet them there.

01

Research

Patient experience was becoming a healthcare platform decision

My research connected a fast-growing healthcare contact-center market with a change in where service work happens: inside clinical systems rather than a conventional agent desktop.

Platform approach

Meet healthcare workers inside their systems of record

Rather than asking care teams to switch tools, the contact center comes into the EHR they already use.

02

The experience problem

Care teams were switching between systems to serve one patient

Every call or message meant moving between the contact-center interface and the patient's record, and routine requests still needed a person to complete them by hand.

01

Two working surfaces

Voice controls and patient context lived in separate interfaces, adding navigation and context switching.

Outcome: one working surface
02

Manual follow-through

AI could understand a patient's request, but completing it meant acting in the EHR with the right permissions and human handoff.

Outcome: AI agents that finish the task
03

Trust and governance

Healthcare data demands security review, audit and clear boundaries for what automation may do.

Outcome: governed automation
03

What launched

Two public launches brought the contact center into Epic

Launched

Epic CTI connector

Contact-center controls inside Epic: agent login and state, voice controls, transfer, conference, recording and click-to-dial.

Launched

AI agent automation with Epic

AI agents that fulfil routine patient requests through Epic, with handoff to a person when needed.

Showcased

HIMSS 2026

Demonstrated AI-powered customer experience for healthcare in the Cisco AI Pavilion and at the Webex CX demo booth.

04

My product leadership

I made healthcare a deliberate part of the connector strategy

Healthcare grew inside the CRM connector portfolio I own. I made healthcare desktop connector growth one of the pillars of the strategy I presented to Directors, VPs and the GM, which executives then presented on stage at WebexOne 2026.

Discover

Define the vertical need

Mapped healthcare personas, EHR workflows and the capabilities care teams need across the patient journey.

Prioritize

Invest in healthcare

Made healthcare connector growth a pillar of the portfolio strategy, alongside AI for desktop connectors.

Deliver

Bring it to Epic

Owned Epic and athenahealth within the connector portfolio, from requirements through security review and launch.

Showcase

Tell the story

Represented Webex Contact Center at HIMSS 2026, demonstrating AI-powered customer experience for healthcare.
