About me

I build serious products without taking curiosity out of the process.

I’m Soundarya, a product manager working at the intersection of enterprise AI, developer platforms, and human-centered design.

I have spent my career translating between people who experience a problem, engineers who understand its technical edges, and businesses that need the solution to work at scale.

The throughline

My career makes more sense as a connected system than a ladder.

Each chapter gave me a different way to understand products. HCI taught me to observe. Engineering taught me to build. Developer experience taught me to design for ecosystems. Product management taught me to turn all three into direction.

  1. 01

    Electronics and communication engineering

    I found my way into interfaces.

    I started creating graphical interfaces during my undergraduate degree and realized I cared as much about how people used technology as how the technology worked.

  2. 02

    Human-computer interaction at RIT

    I learned to begin with people.

    Graduate work in research, interaction design, and usability gave me the methods to move from assumptions to evidence.

  3. 03

    Engineering and design at Cisco

    I learned what it takes to make an idea real.

    I built front ends in Angular and React, spent a year as a UX designer running research and prototyping, then led a React and GraphQL team. I developed a lasting respect for implementation details.

  4. 04

    Developer experience and AI products

    I now lead platforms other teams build on.

    Today I shape developer ecosystems and conversational AI products across APIs, partner integrations, cloud and on-premises environments, and enterprise workflows.

03
